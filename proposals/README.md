# proposals/

Staging area for evolution amendment batches (`docs/evolve.md` — internal procedure; `/evolve`
was demoted at 0.3.4). The scheduled background run writes a draft `<proposal-id>.md` here and
emails it to the Human. Nothing here is applied to the constitution until the email GRANT
router applies it on an explicit grant. Drafts are kept as an audit trail of what was proposed
(and what was granted vs. dropped).
